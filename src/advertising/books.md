# Books — programmatic advertising

Commercial or gated. Not downloaded; links + one-line summaries only.

## Academic / survey

- **Introduction to Computational Advertising** — Andrei Broder & Vanja Josifovski. Stanford lecture notes (MS&E 239 / CS 369X). The canonical academic starting point. Course pages drift; search "Broder Josifovski computational advertising Stanford" for the current mirror.
- **Computational Advertising: Techniques for Targeting Relevant Ads** — Kushal Dave & Vasudeva Varma. *Foundations and Trends in Information Retrieval*, 2014. https://www.nowpublishers.com/article/Details/INR-045 — concise academic survey of sponsored search + contextual ads.
- **Introduction to Online Advertising** — Ari Juels / Mohammad Mahdian lecture series. Aggregated course material on online-ad economics.
- **AdKDD Proceedings** — Springer publishes annual volumes. AdKDD 2025 (Toronto) is the most recent. https://www.adkdd.org — workshop site has accepted-paper lists each year.

## Industry / practitioner

- **The Online Advertising Playbook** — Joe Plummer, Steve Rappaport, Taddy Hall, Robert Barocci (IAB / Wiley, 2007). Business-side foundation, aging but still cited.
- **Programmatic Advertising: The Successful Kick-off to Media Buying Automation** — Oliver Busch (ed.), Springer 2016. Practitioner overview of the DSP/SSP ecosystem. https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-319-25023-6
- **Targeted: How Technology Is Revolutionizing Advertising** — Mike Smith (former CRO of Forbes), 2014. Industry history + RTB mechanics, written for non-engineers.

## Critical / market lens

- **Subprime Attention Crisis** — Tim Hwang (FSG Originals, 2020). Argues programmatic advertising is structurally similar to the mortgage bubble. Direct relevance to inventory-quality / fraud / IVT work.

## How to use

Skim Busch + Smith first for vocabulary; Broder/Josifovski for the
computational framing; then go straight to `papers/arxiv/` for the
technical depth. Hwang is the read for why any of this matters.
