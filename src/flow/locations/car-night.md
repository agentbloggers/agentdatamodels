# Location — car at night

Interior front seat of a dark sedan or small SUV, parked curbside in a
city at night. Gemmah is in the driver's seat or passenger seat —
**not driving** (for safety plausibility; she can be parked).

## Lighting

- Dominant wash: **red / magenta** across the face (stoplight through
  the windshield, or a nearby neon sign, or an interior LED accent
  strip). The reference image for this location has a strong red
  gradient falling top-to-bottom on the face.
- Secondary: cool blue-white highlights behind from city street lamps
  and passing headlights (bokeh, shallow depth of field).
- No harsh key light. No ring-light look.

## Composition

- **Selfie framing** — camera slightly below eye level, ~arm's-length
  distance. Phone in portrait for 9:16, landscape for 16:9 (same
  location, two camera rotations).
- Subject centered; headliner of the car visible above the hair.
- **Seatbelt across left shoulder** visible — grounds the scene.
- Passenger seat and the interior ceiling curves visible in frame.
- Rear side windows show blurred city skyline (shallow DOF, large
  bokeh circles).

## Motion

- Very subtle — Gemmah may adjust posture, look over her shoulder
  once, or glance down at her phone briefly. **No large gestures.**
  Veo 3.1 handles micro-motion well and this reinforces the
  "captured, not performed" feel.
- Camera is hand-held — mild sway, not stable tripod.

## Audio bed

- Faint city ambient: distant traffic, one passing horn, muted muffled
  dance music behind (no recognizable melody — Veo will hallucinate
  lyrics if you specify songs).
- Voice delivered slightly breathy, close to the mic — matches the
  "talking to your phone in the car" vibe.

## Veo prompt block (appended after character brief)

```
SETTING: Interior front seat of a dark sedan at night, parked in a
city. Subject bathed in red/magenta ambient light from the top of
frame, cool blue-white bokeh from passing headlights and city lamps
behind her through the rear windows. Seatbelt visible across her
left shoulder. Shallow depth of field. Hand-held selfie framing,
camera slightly below eye level. Faint ambient city audio — distant
traffic, no music with lyrics.
```

## Common failure modes for this location

Watch the evaluate rubric for:

- Seatbelt warping into her shoulder or doubling
- Steering wheel morphing — if visible, keep it out of the primary
  focal plane
- Reflections in the windshield glass showing extra faces (common
  Veo bug)
- Red light inverted to blue — if the magenta wash goes cool, reject
